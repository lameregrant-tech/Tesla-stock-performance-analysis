Tesla Stock Analysis


How much did Tesla’s stock increase from its lowest closing price to its highest closing price?

Analysis:
This analysis examined Tesla’s historical closing prices to determine how much the stock increased from its lowest closing price to its highest closing price. The results conveyed that Tesla’s lowest closing price was $3.16, while on the other end, its highest closing price reached $1,229.91. This represents a $1,226.75 increase, or in other words, approximately 38,821.20% from the lowest closing price to the highest closing price. These results demonstrate the abundant growth in Tesla’s stock price over the period of 2010 to 2022, and they highlight the substantial price movement that the company experienced as its market value increased.

Interpretation:
Due to the fact that this analysis utilizes split-adjusted historical pricing, specifically accounting for the 5-for-1 stock split in 2020, this percentage accurately reflects the true, exponential return an investor would have experienced during this timeframe. As a result of using adjusted closing data rather than raw prices, the artificial distortion caused by stock splits is removed, which confirms this 38,821.20% growth as a genuine representation of the company's increase in market value.
